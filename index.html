SocialUploader

SocialUploader is a small private desktop tool. It uploads videos that its owner made to the owner's own YouTube channels, sets their thumbnails, titles, descriptions and schedules, and posts a pinned comment on them.

It is not offered to the public, and it only ever acts on channels its owner has signed in to.

See the privacy policy and terms of service.
